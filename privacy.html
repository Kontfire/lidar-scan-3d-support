Privacy Policy for LiDAR Scan 3D

Data Collection

LiDAR Scan 3D does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with the developer or third parties.

Camera, LiDAR and TrueDepth

LiDAR Scan 3D uses the device camera, LiDAR sensor and TrueDepth system to provide 3D scanning features. Sensor data is processed on the user's device for the purpose of creating and displaying 3D scans.

Saved Scans

Scans and exported files created by the user are stored locally on the device unless the user explicitly chooses to export or share them.

Third-Party Services

LiDAR Scan 3D currently does not use advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics services.
