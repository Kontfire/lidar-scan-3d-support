Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

LiDAR Scanner is designed to process 3D scans primarily on your device. We value your privacy and aim to collect only the information necessary to provide app functionality and user support.

1. 3D Scans and Camera Data

3D scans, camera images used for scanning, depth information, meshes, textures, and exported 3D files are processed locally on your device.

The app does not automatically upload your scans or 3D models to our servers.

Your scans leave your device only when you explicitly choose to share, export, or send them using features provided by iOS.

2. Feedback and Support

If you choose to use the in-app Feedback feature, the following information may be transmitted:

  • The feedback message you enter
  • Up to three screenshots or images that you voluntarily attach
  • App version and build number
  • iOS version
  • Device model
  • Selected app language

This information is collected only when you intentionally press the Send button in the Feedback screen.

We do not intentionally collect your name, phone number, precise location, advertising identifier, Apple ID, or other persistent user identifiers through the Feedback feature.

3. How Feedback Information Is Used

Feedback information is used solely to:

  • Respond to support requests
  • Investigate bugs and technical problems
  • Improve scanning quality and application functionality
  • Improve the overall user experience

Feedback information is not used for advertising, profiling, or cross-app tracking.

4. Service Providers

The Feedback feature uses third-party infrastructure providers to securely process and deliver support messages.

Cloudflare Workers is used to securely receive and process feedback requests.

Resend is used to deliver feedback messages and optional attachments to our support email inbox.

These providers process information only as necessary to provide these services and are subject to their own privacy and data protection policies.

5. Data Retention

Feedback messages may be retained in our support mailbox for as long as reasonably necessary to investigate the issue, provide support, maintain a record of technical problems, and improve the application.

Our email delivery provider may temporarily retain email and log data according to its own retention policies.

We do not maintain a separate user profile or advertising database using feedback submissions.

6. Tracking and Advertising

LiDAR Scanner does not use feedback information for advertising or tracking.

The app does not use the feedback system to track your activity across other companies' apps or websites.

7. User Choice

Using the Feedback feature is entirely optional.

Screenshots and images are transmitted only when you explicitly select them for inclusion in your feedback.

You can continue using the scanning features of the app without submitting feedback.

8. Data Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of a feedback submission by contacting us. Because the Feedback feature does not require a user account, you may need to provide sufficient information such as the approximate submission date and message content so that the submission can be identified.

9. Children's Privacy

The app is not designed to intentionally collect personal information from children through the Feedback feature.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app functionality or data practices change. The latest version will always be available at this page.

3D taramalar, tarama sırasında kullanılan kamera görüntüleri, derinlik bilgileri, mesh verileri, texture'lar ve dışa aktarılan 3D dosyalar cihazınız üzerinde işlenir.

Uygulama taramalarınızı veya 3D modellerinizi otomatik olarak sunucularımıza yüklemez.

Bu dosyalar yalnızca sizin paylaşma veya dışa aktarma işlemini açıkça seçmeniz durumunda cihazınızdan çıkar.

  • Uygulama sürümü ve build numarası
  • iOS sürümü
  • Cihaz modeli
  • Seçili uygulama dili

Bu bilgiler yalnızca Geri Bildirim ekranında Gönder butonuna bilinçli olarak bastığınızda iletilir.

Geri Bildirim özelliği aracılığıyla adınızı, telefon numaranızı, kesin konumunuzu, reklam kimliğinizi, Apple ID'nizi veya başka kalıcı kullanıcı tanımlayıcılarını bilerek toplamıyoruz.
